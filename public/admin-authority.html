TENSEI.EXE · MOVE COLLECTION AUTHORITY

What this does

It moves the update authority of your collection from the old (compromised) wallet to your new wallet in one transaction. The new wallet pays the fee and the old wallet only signs, so no SOL is sent to the old wallet for a bot to take. Never enter a seed phrase here. This page never asks for one.

You need Phantom with both accounts in the same Phantom (add the old one as an imported account if needed) and at least 0.01 SOL in the NEW account. Do this soon: the old key is exposed.
